## 2. User's proposed ordered algorithm — exact intent, with needed clarifications

Applies to *derived institution prizes* (Best School/Academy/Club/City/State), aggregating top **K selected individuals in one tournament**; NOT to a genuine team-vs-team event with match points. Respect existing configured gender minimums and roster eligibility; do not alter individual allocation engine.

Proposed ranking within one prize group:
- **Primary — Total selected player points, DESC:** \(P(T)=\sum_{i\in S_T}p_i\), where \(S_T\) is the validated, eligible selection of exactly K players and \(p_i\) is each person's true imported tournament points. If any required points missing/non-finite: **BLOCK automatic monetary allocation / publishing for that affected team group**, show actionable organizer diagnostic; do not coerce to zero or substitute Rank Sum.
- **Tie-break 1 — Sorted selected-player score vector, lexicographic DESC:** sort the K **selected players' individual points** highest to lowest. Compare highest score; if equal compare second, third, etc. For 3+ teams, independently partition remaining ties at each comparison; do not omit tied teams or sort by player name/rank in the deciding vector. A=(7,5) and B=(6,6), both 12, yields **A first under this PROPOSED rule** (7>6). **This intentionally favors a standout performer over a balanced equal-total roster; it is an event-specific product rule, not a claimed FIDE/AICF mandate.** Selection of K itself currently uses rank ascending plus configured gender slots; confirm whether selection should remain rank-based or change to points-based before implementing.
- **Tie-break 2 — sum of selected players' Buchholz Cut-1, DESC** (as supplied and validated from the imported Swiss-Manager XLS/XLSX): \(BC1(T)=\sum_{i\in S_T}BH\text{-}C1_i\). This is a **new team-level aggregate of individual Buchholz Cut-1 values**, not a standardized FIDE championship team tie-break. Do NOT sum only the K smallest/opponent values, and do NOT calculate an individual player's BH-C1 using team participants; accept a clearly mapped source value with provenance. Missing/ambiguous/non-final cells MUST block automatic resolution, not become 0.
- **Tie-break 3 — sum of the selected players' 'second tie-break', DESC**, \(TB2(T)=\sum_{i\in S_T}tb2_i\), **ONLY after the user identifies which source column and definition is intended**. It might mean Swiss-Manager's configurable second listed tie-break, normal Buchholz (BH), Sonneborn-Berger (SB), or another field. These are NOT interchangeable and neither column ordinal nor numeric direction is universal. Resolve label, source-column mapping, ascending/descending meaning and event rules before treating this as a criterion. Use only a homogeneous tie-break measure for every participant in a group.
- **Still equal:** mark involved places **TIE — RESOLUTION REQUIRED**; organizer/arbiter may offer a **play-off only if tournament regulations permit and define** the format (time control, pairings, color, schedule, which places, recording results), or use another pre-published method / allowed shared-place outcome. No silent automatic playoff scheduling or awarding the first-place monetary prize while unresolved.

**Event-rule priority:** The above proposed ordered scheme is valid for a new event **only when its applicable tournament regulations define or authorize it**. If its published rules say something else, do not overwrite them with the platform default. If no rules establish this scheme, stop for authorized organizer/arbiter adjudication. Do not retroactively rerank already-finalized prizes.

**Specific FIDE March 2026 caveat (C.07 articles 4.1.1 and 4.2, independently rechecked):** When an event's tie-break list needs completion, the Chief Arbiter selects permitted additional tie-breaks and publishes them **before the tournament**. That is the stated FIDE-regulation fallback, **not** an automatic playoff mandate and **not** a license for Prize Manager to choose winners by name or lots without tournament-specific authority. The owner's preferred **pending organizer/arbiter review** is a safe *application state while lawful resolution is obtained*, not a claim that indefinite pending/manual adjudication itself replaces an applicable FIDE final-classification rule. ## 6A. Additional official 2025 results cross-check — TB number is not a meaning

The public [Chess-Results standing for the 44th National Team Chess Championship 2025](https://s2.chess-results.com/Test/tnr1127680.aspx?lan=11) explicitly identifies **TB1 = Matchpoints, TB2 = Extended Direct Encounter for teams (Matchpoints), TB3 = Game Points, TB4 = EMGSB, TB5 = SSSC**. This is a real example where **'TB2' is neither Buchholz nor Sonneborn-Berger**. It concerns an actual head-to-head team championship, so it is **not a rule to copy** for derived school/academy awards. It strengthens the ingestion requirement to persist the *actual metric name, direction and tournament-rule provenance* rather than trusting an ordinal `TB2` header.

The [AICF 2013 published regulations](https://aicf.in/tie-break-regulations/) list **Buchholz Cut-1, Buchholz Cut-2, Sonneborn-Berger, Buchholz Median** for individual Swiss championships, while specifying **match points and game/opponent points** for separate national-team championships. The differing lists reinforce that the user's proposed **sum of individual BH-C1** is a custom team-award method, not a default mandated by AICF. Current FIDE C.07 is [the revised regulation effective March 2026](https://www.fide.com/fide-reminds-organizers-and-arbiters-of-updated-play-off-and-tie-break-regulations-effective-march-1-2026/). ## 6B. Verified 2026 federation comparison — why the team format matters

This is **external competition-rule evidence**, not a new owner-approved Prize Manager rule:

1. [FIDE Chess Olympiad 2026 official competition regulations, §§4.5.1 and Appendix 2.I](https://handbook.fide.com/files/handbook/Olympiad2026MainCompetition.pdf): actual *head-to-head team matches* rank first by **team Match Points (2/1/0)**, then on equal match points by the specified opponent-weighted score based on game points and opponents' final match points (with one exclusion), then **game points**, then **sum of selected opponents' match points**; a final unresolved tie **remains tied under that event's own rules**. This confirms that a fixed global Rank Sum is **not** a FIDE-wide default and also illustrates why different events can explicitly permit shared places.
2. [AICF's 2013 published tie-break guidance](https://aicf.in/tie-break-regulations/) separately states **Buchholz Cut-1, Buchholz Cut-2, Sonneborn-Berger, Buchholz Median** for individual Swiss national competitions but **Match Points / Game Points / opponent-based measures** for national *head-to-head team championships*. The guidance is historical and competition-specific, NOT a universal formula for Best School/Club derived awards in 2026. Actual competition circulars can differ; apply the specific event's published provisions.
3. [AICF-hosted Maharathi Classical 2025 regulations](https://aicf.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/1st-Maharathi-Classical-1.pdf) provide a more comparable **derived Best Academy/Best School** example: **average points of top four players**. For a fixed K, averages and sums rank identically; this supports keeping actual player points as primary, **but supplies no authorization for the user's proposed top-scorer/BH-C1/TB2 fallback order**.
4. [Swiss-Manager/Chess-Results FAQ — exported tie-break columns](https://chess-results.com/Test/FAQ.aspx?id=5872&lan=7) documents that Excel output can omit all but the first tie-break until the export columns are explicitly enabled. Therefore the importer must not assume the second metric is present or decode `TB2` as one universal statistic.
5. [FIDE C.07 effective 1 March 2026, Articles 2–4](https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/TieBreakRegulations032026) says published event rules specify the ordered tie-breaks and playoff provisions; Article 4.2 ordinarily resorts to drawing lots if the list is exhausted **unless event regulations expressly permit unresolved ties**. **Prize Manager must never implement an automatic lottery, custom score-vector, or mandatory playoff in place of applicable published regulations.** A pending organizer/arbiter review is an application safety hold until a lawful rule-driven resolution exists.

**Practical contrast:** The current engine's derived school/academy grouping aggregates selected individual player scores from a Swiss ranking list. It has no match-by-match opposing-team schedule or Match Point record. Copying AICF/FIDE head-to-head team tiebreaks directly would fabricate unavailable inputs. A source-grounded **event-specific prize policy** is the right boundary, and the user-proposed lexicographic score vector remains an explicitly custom rule pending approval.
