# Prize Manager — Referral Program

*How it works, in plain language. Share this with organizers.*

---

## The short version

Invite another organizer. When they upgrade a tournament to Pro, **you get a free or discounted tournament.** So does the person who invited *you*. And the person who invited *them*.

Three levels deep. Everyone in the chain benefits from one upgrade at the bottom.

---

## What you get

| Your position | Your reward |
|---|---|
| You invited them directly | **100% off** — a completely free Pro tournament |
| They were invited by someone you invited | **50% off** |
| One step further down | **25% off** |

Every coupon is valid for **365 days** and can be used **once**.

---

## How to invite someone

1. Go to **Account**.
2. Click **Copy referral signup link**. That gives you the full link, which looks like:
   ```
   https://prize-manager.com/auth?mode=signup&ref=REF-1C363C3D
   ```
   Your referral code on its own (`REF-1C363C3D`) is shown just above it, if you would rather share that.

When they sign up through that link and confirm their email, the referral is recorded automatically.

**Important:** they must sign up using your link and then confirm their email in the same sitting. Clicking the confirmation link is what records the referral. If they sign up now and confirm days later — or sign in somewhere else in between — the connection will not register, and it cannot be added afterwards.

---

## When do I actually get the coupon?

Not at signup. **When they pay for a Pro tournament.**

Signing up costs nothing and triggers nothing. The reward is issued the moment someone in your chain upgrades a real tournament.

Two things count as upgrading:
- Paying for Pro by UPI
- Redeeming a Pro coupon on a tournament

The second one matters more than it looks — **a chain keeps paying out.** When someone uses a free coupon you earned them, that itself rewards the people above them.

---

## A worked example

Four organizers: **Asha → Bhavna → Chetan → Divya**, each invited by the one before.

**Divya upgrades a tournament for ₹500.** Instantly:

- **Chetan** (invited Divya) → 100% off — a free tournament
- **Bhavna** (invited Chetan) → 50% off
- **Asha** (invited Bhavna) → 25% off

**Then Chetan uses his free coupon.** That is an upgrade too, so:

- **Bhavna** (invited Chetan) → another 100% off
- **Asha** (invited Bhavna) → another 50% off

One ₹500 payment at the bottom put **five coupons** into the hands of three people.

## What Pro costs, and when you need it

Pricing is by tournament size:

| Players | Price |
|---|---|
| Up to 150 | **Free** |
| 151 – 500 | ₹500 |
| Over 500 | ₹1,000 |

Small tournaments are free and always will be. You only pay once a tournament passes 150 players, and a coupon can cover that entirely.

---

## Where to find your coupons

**Account** shows your referral code, who has joined through it, and the coupons you have earned.

Enter a coupon code on the tournament's payment page under **Apply Coupon**. A 100% coupon takes the price to ₹0 and unlocks Pro immediately.

Each coupon works once. If a code is refused, it has most likely already been used.

---

## Common questions

**Can I invite myself with a second email?**
No. Self-referral is blocked.

**Can I give my coupon to someone else?**
No. Each coupon is tied to the person who earned it.

**Does my coupon expire?**
After 365 days, or once you have used it. Each one works a single time.

**What if my friend signs up but never runs a big tournament?**
Then no coupon is issued. Rewards are triggered by an actual upgrade, not by signups. This keeps the program honest.

**How deep does it go?**
Three levels. Beyond that, nothing is issued.

**Can I earn more than one coupon?**
Yes. Every qualifying upgrade anywhere in your chain issues a new one.

---

## Why the program is built this way

Rewards are paid on **real upgrades**, never on signups. That means the program cannot be farmed by creating accounts, and every coupon issued corresponds to a tournament that genuinely reached Pro size.

It is designed to reward organizers who bring in other *active* organizers — not to reward volume.
